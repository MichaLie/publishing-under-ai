# Changelog

## 1.1 — 13 November 2026

- The page now opens with its take-away and a "Start here" guide for different readers; sections renamed (What changed · Where people disagree · Proposed fixes · All sources · About).
- Fault lines F4, F5 and F6 now use the same direction as the others: the more cautious side on the left (−2), the side that lets AI in or opens up the system on the right (+2). Their scores were sign-flipped accordingly; no placement changed in substance. Questions where no source argues a side outright now say so.
- New "About this collection" section with scope, corrections route, how-to-cite and AI-use statement; links to GitHub and issues on every page; link-preview images for social media.
- 16 new sources (141 in total): fabricated-citation audits across 2.5 million biomedical papers (The Lancet) and across preprint servers; private AI-disclosure data from BMJ and JAMA Network journal submissions; hidden-prompt manipulation of AI review of medical manuscripts; a survey of medical-journal reviewers; the AAAI-26 AI review pilot; ICML 2026's randomized policy experiment and its enforcement of no-LLM pledges; paper-mill screening in cancer research; language bias in computer-science peer review; the costs of being a non-native English speaker in science; AI–AI bias; the Cochrane/Campbell/JBI/CEE position on AI in evidence synthesis; arXiv's peer-review requirement for CS reviews and position papers; and openRxiv's approach to AI at bioRxiv and medRxiv.
- Every summary flagged in the November review was re-checked against its source and corrected where it said more than the source, and trailing caveats were rewritten as plain statements. Where full texts were available, quotes were re-checked against them; one quote that could not be found in any accessible version of its source (Guo et al., Cell) was replaced with a sentence from the abstract, and its fault-line placement removed. PubMed IDs added for every record Europe PMC indexes. Renault et al. now cites the published PNAS version (10.1073/pnas.2618638123); one Retraction Watch excerpt replaced; two excerpt spacing errors fixed; quote-source labels reworded ("Full text", "Publisher page").
- Placements: each underlying claim is placed once, so posts that echo an already-placed study stay unplaced (the Rust thread, the Mollick post and the Yamada preprint, whose experiment the Nature paper by Lu et al. reports); argued posts by named researchers are now placed on both sides (Rosen, Doshi, Briggs, Yona), as is the Heart editorial; AI-authorship experiments moved off F1, which is about prose versus executable records; the Barnett and Spick blog moved off F6, which it does not address.
- Measured shifts SW3–SW7 updated with the new evidence and rewritten for readability.
- Essay: TL;DR now keeps the 89% and 0.1% figures apart (different corpora); ICMJE update now dated (January 2026); shorter colophon.
- Removed internal collection notes from the page and from `data.json`.
